Privacy Policy

How Kinetic Enterprise handles information you share with us through our website and communications.

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Introduction

Kinetic Enterprise (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website or contact us about our IT services and products.

Information we collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide — such as your name, email address, phone number, and message content — when you use our contact form or communicate with us. We may also receive basic technical data such as browser type, device information, and pages visited through standard web analytics or server logs.

How we use information

We use the information we collect to respond to enquiries, provide requested services, improve our website and communications, and meet legitimate business or legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing of information

We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate our website or business (for example, hosting or email delivery), solely as needed to perform those services. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights and safety.

Data retention & security

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required by law. We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to help protect information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided to us, subject to applicable law. To make a request, contact us using the details on our Contact page.

Third-party links

Our website may link to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third parties.

Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision. Continued use of our website after changes means you acknowledge the updated policy.
